=== Short description (max 80) ===

=== Full description ===
Need a handyman? We bring them to your door! 🔧
Whether you need a plumber, electrician, carpenter, painter, AC technician, mechanic or any other tradesman, find the right person, check their ratings, and pick the closest one to you — easily.

📍 Find the nearest handyman
• Search by trade, service or name
• Set your location by GPS, manually (governorate → city → area) or on the map
• See workers on a map with distances
• Filters: distance, rating, available now, verified, visit fee

⭐ Check ratings and choose with confidence
• Real reviews from customers who completed a job
• "Verified ✓" badge for workers reviewed by our team
• Top-rated and most-completed badges
• Photos of previous work

📲 Request a service and track it step by step
• Book a time or accept a proposed time
• Track status: accepted → on the way → started → done
• In-app chat with photos and location, plus direct call or WhatsApp

🚨 Need a handyman NOW
An emergency button that instantly notifies the nearest available workers.

🔧 Are you a handyman?
• Register your trade, area and work photos
• Receive nearby jobs and emergency requests
• Track your stats, ratings and wallet
• Small commission on completed jobs only, paid via InstaPay

🛡️ Safety & privacy
• Your phone number is shown to the worker only after they accept
• ID details are visible only to the admin team
• Delete your account anytime from inside the app

Free for customers.
